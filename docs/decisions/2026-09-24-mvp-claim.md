# MVP claim

- **Topic:** Master plan ch. 1.2: the one claim the MVP must prove
- **Options considered:** A pipeline only · B full loop (finds, proves, fast, few fixes, grows) · C value (surgeons find it useful)
- **Choice:** **B + C.** B is proven by tests against sealed answer keys. C is **shown** (not proven) by a research-grounded **stand-in surgeon** played by the team.
- **Reason:** Karthi: the MVP is an example/demo, so the team can play the surgeon role, backed by thorough research. This lets the demo show value to all three audiences.
- **Claude's caution:**
  - A stand-in can demonstrate usefulness but can't prove it for real surgeons → labelled 🎭 in every demo; real proof comes in the pilot
  - The team also builds the factory → blind review needs a clear rule (to confirm)
  - Medical content from research must cite its source (📎 rule)
- **Sub-points (confirmed same day):**
  - Stand-in = **Karthi** (human in the loop: taps + voice); **Claude** builds the cited research pack. (Other options: Claude only · someone else)
  - Blind review = **the reviewer never opens scripts or keys** for cases they review; F1 runs automatically and seals keys; numbers come from the simulated reviewer. (Other options: accept non-blind · separate case sets)
- **Decided by:** Karthi · 24 Sep 2026
