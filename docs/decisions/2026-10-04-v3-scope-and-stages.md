Dates are indicative and driven by ethics approval. Progress is measured by exits, not dates.

## Never, for a 3-person company

- A "competitive parity" frame.
- Quality dashboards and surgeon scoring.
- 7 storage modes.
- HL7/FHIR.
- Our own capture hardware: use partner or hospital recorders.
- In-app billing.
- Non-Japanese coding systems.
- Operative-note generation.
- Safety / near-miss (QI) analytics.

KO/ZH/HI translations are **frozen, not deleted**.
