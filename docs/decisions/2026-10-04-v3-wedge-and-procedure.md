# v3 ch. 1: wedge and first procedure pack

- **Topic:** Which use case the product enters the market with, and which procedure is designed first.

## Wedge

- **Options considered:**
  - **A. Archive narration first.** The expert narrates their own archive videos → a department living manual → juniors' pre-case study. Prospective OR capture becomes phase 2.
  - **B. Prospective OR capture first** (the CEO's OR edition: capture during surgery + post-op debrief).
  - **C. Both from day 1.**
- **Choice: A.**
  - The prospective path is **not dropped**. It is phase 2 on the same engine, card and review screen.
  - **Roles:** contributor = senior surgeon / proctor · weekly user = juniors / fellows · buyer = department head.
- **Reasons:**
  - Video-cued recall yields **2–4× more** recollections than free recall (RS2 §2.1).
  - Experts omit **34–77%** of their decisions when unprompted (RS2 §1.1).
  - No capture hardware is needed.
  - No OR-staff voices, so the ethics route is simpler (RS6 §4b).
  - Juniors have a weekly need: 89% study after hours, and the 2026 JUA/JSER robotic guideline requires video study (RS7 §1).
  - A department licence (≈ ¥1.5–2M/yr) stays under the no-tender threshold (RS7 §3).
  - The earliest lawful real case is ≈ Jan–Mar 2027 either way (RS6 §4b).
  - B needs a capture PC, an integrator and consent from every OR staff member, which puts it at ≈ mid/late 2027.

## First procedure pack

- **Options considered:** adrenalectomy first, then RARP · RARP first · stay generic until the partner is confirmed.
- **Choice: adrenalectomy first, then RARP.** The architecture covers both from day 1, with side, approach, robot platform and indication as variants.
- **Reasons:**
  - Adrenalectomy is short, has ≈ 6 phases and gives outcomes within days.
  - It is the CEO's demo procedure and the likely partner's signature strength: Chiba University does 49–65 a year, top tier nationally (RS1, NDB FY2024; partner unconfirmed).
  - Certification-video culture already exists for it (RS1).
  - RARP (27,674 cases a year, 94.5% robotic) is the business and must follow early (RS1, RS7).
  - Robotic adrenalectomy alone is only ≈ 438 cases a year, so it is not a business on its own (RS7).
- **Standing rule:** the procedure follows the clinical partner. If the partner is not Chiba University, does fewer than 20 adrenalectomies a year, or its lead surgeon is a prostate surgeon, switch to RARP first (RS1 §recommendation).
- **Laterality rule:** left/right anatomy is enforced by ontology validation rules. The CEO's seed cards for a *left* adrenalectomy say "expose the IVC plane first", which is a right-sided step.
